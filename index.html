02

Descent modes

Choosing a descent mode for a situation is important. We need to consider things such as:

  • ATC requirements – Speed and “level by” clearances
  • Airspace limitations – Max rate of climb or descent within a certain heights from a cleared level
  • Lateral navigation mode – HDG or NAV
  • Programmed data – Descent speeds and wind data

Route knowledge (observed or read), will MASSIVELY feed into your expectation. OMC briefs and Fly Guide knowledge are invaluable.

READ THE ROOM – What can you hear other aircraft on frequency getting? This will feed into the route knowledge expectation.

If your plan isn’t working, do something about it. A problem fixed early will easy to overcome than one fixed later, as you are likely to deviate further away from the ideal flight path.
